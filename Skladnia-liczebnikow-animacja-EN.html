The sections follow the rules on the HPP B1 36 reference card. Cards OJP 8.7–8.11 from Odkrywamy język polski provide supporting examples. This presentation practises short numeral–noun phrases in the constructions covered by the card. It does not cover the full case system: compare the dative dwojgu dzieciom and the locative o dwojgu dzieciach.
